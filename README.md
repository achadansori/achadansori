### 🤖 About Me

R&D Engineer at **PT Aldzama** (Gresik, Indonesia), building embedded control systems for **industrial demolition robots** — from schematic and PCB design through real-time firmware and electro-hydraulic control.

My background spans **marine robotics, offshore operations, smart manufacturing, aquaculture tech, and demolition robotics**. I work across hardware and software, turning prototypes into systems that hold up in the field.

- 🔧 Currently: embedded control & electro-hydraulic systems for demolition robots
- 🌊 Roots: AUV/ASV autonomy with the Penship EEPIS Marine Robotics Team
- 🛢️ Offshore: led recovery of a non-operational ROV and piloted it on an offshore hull inspection survey
- 🎯 Goal: robotics for Indonesian heavy industry — offshore, mining, construction
